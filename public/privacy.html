Privacy Policy

Workout Timer · Last updated 24 August 2026

Workout Timer is an interval training timer. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what control you have over it. We do not sell your data, show you ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

What we collect

  • Your email address — used only to create and sign in to your account. If you sign in with Google, we receive the email address associated with that Google account.
  • Your workouts — the names, sections, rounds, work and rest durations, and sound choices you create, so they sync across your devices.
  • Spotify connection data — only if you choose to connect Spotify. We store an access token and refresh token so playback can start when your workout does, and we read your display name, subscription tier, and playlists to show them in the app.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health or fitness records, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics.

Where your data is stored

Account details, workouts, and Spotify tokens are stored in our database hosted by Supabase. Access is restricted so that only your account can read or modify your own rows. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and authentication tokens on your device are kept in the operating system's secure keychain.

Third parties

  • Supabase — hosts our database and authentication.
  • Spotify — only if you connect it. Your use of Spotify is governed by Spotify's own privacy policy. You can disconnect Spotify at any time from within the app, which deletes the stored tokens.
  • Google — only if you use Google sign-in, and only to verify your identity.

We do not share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account from inside the app: open Account from the workout library, then choose Delete Account. This immediately and irreversibly removes your account, all saved workouts, and any stored Spotify tokens. You can also disconnect Spotify on its own without deleting your account.

Children

Workout Timer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
