Workout Timer

Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 August 2026

The short version

  • We collect your email, the workouts you create, and — only if you connect it — your Spotify authorisation.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is ever sold or rented.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, at any time.

Workout Timer is an interval training timer for iOS. This policy explains what the app collects, why it needs each piece, and how to get rid of all of it. It covers the Workout Timer mobile app and its companion website.

What we collect

Account

Email address

Collected when you create an account. If you sign in with Google we receive the email address on that Google account, and nothing else from your Google profile.

Why
To identify your account and let you sign back in.
Kept
Until you delete your account.

Content

The workouts you create

Workout names, sections, rounds, work and rest durations, and your chosen transition sounds.

Why
So your workouts are saved between sessions and available on any device you sign in to.
Kept
Until you delete the workout, or your account.

Optional

Spotify connection

Only if you choose to connect Spotify. We store the access and refresh tokens Spotify issues us, and read your display name, subscription tier, and playlists so we can show them and start playback when a workout begins. We never see your Spotify password.

Why
To start your chosen playlist when a workout starts.
Kept
Until you disconnect Spotify or delete your account, whichever comes first.

What we don't collect

No location. No contacts, photos, or microphone access. No health or fitness records — the app never reads from or writes to Apple Health. No advertising identifiers, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, and no payment details.

We do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone.

Where your data is stored

Your account, workouts, and Spotify authorisation are stored in a database hosted by Supabase, which processes this data on our behalf. Access is enforced at the database level, so each account can only read and modify its own records.

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). On your device, the credentials that keep you signed in are held in the iOS keychain rather than ordinary app storage.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase — hosts the database and handles sign-in.
  • Spotify — only if you connect it. What Spotify does with your listening is governed by Spotify's privacy policy.
  • Google — only if you use Google sign-in, and only to confirm your email address.

No one else receives your data.

Deleting your data

Open Account from your workout library and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, every workout you've saved, and any stored Spotify tokens. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

If you only want to disconnect Spotify, you can do that on its own without deleting anything else — your account and workouts stay put.

Children

Workout Timer isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us their information, email us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where it affects you, say so in the app.
